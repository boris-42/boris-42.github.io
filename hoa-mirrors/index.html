Install

SecurityMan 24 in outdoor convex mirror. Heights to the mirror's center; bearings true north.

Aim it

  1. Mount it loose at the height and bearing given.
  2. A driver sits in a car 30 ft before the turn.
  3. A helper stands where a hidden child would be.
  4. Turn and tilt until the helper is mid-mirror, check from the other alley, tighten.
Notes
  • A child in the mirror is a small moving speck: it shows something is coming, not who.
  • Real field of view is about 80 deg (the listing says 180).
  • The plan is accurate to about 3 ft. Shrubs count as 3 ft tall, trees by their trunks.
  • Pictures are renders of a 3D model, not photos.
