Convex mirrors for the blind turn behind our garages

Where the alley from the street (A1) turns into the alley behind the next building (A2), the corner building hides each approach from the other. Cars meet there at the turn, and kids run out of the walk from the lawn just around the corner.

We propose a 24 in outdoor convex traffic mirror at the turn: on a post on the island by the hydrant (A), on the garage wall across the turn (B), or both. This page shows what a driver sees with each, and how to put them up.

How much of the approach each one sees

Share of the spots on the other approaches (children by the curbs and garages, and cars heading for the turn, every 10 ft out to 90 ft) that the person at the marked spot can see, directly or in a mirror. With both mirrors, each is aimed at one approach (see How to install).

Keep in mind

  • A child in the mirror is small: a moving speck about 1 in tall on the mirror face when you are 30 ft from it. The mirror tells you something is coming, not who.
  • The plan comes from building outlines and the 3D model, accurate to about 3 ft. Shrubs on the islands count as 3 ft tall; trees count by their trunks only.
  • This mirror's real field of view is about 80 degrees (22.5 in lens, 2 in dome). The listing says 180 and the maker's site 90.
  • The map is a plan: it treats the lens by its horizontal middle line and assumes the driver turns their head.

How to install

The mirror: SecurityMan 24 in outdoor convex mirror (24 in frame, 22.5 in lens), with its adjustable bracket (a collar clamp for a post, or an L plate for a wall). Heights are to the center of the mirror. Compass bearings are true north (on an iPhone: Settings, Compass, Use True North).

Aim it on site

  1. Mount the mirror loosely at the height given, facing roughly the bearing given.
  2. One person sits in a car (or stands with their eyes at 3 ft 9 in, a sedan driver's eye) in the lane about 30 ft before the corner, where the map's driver marker is.
  3. A helper stands where a child would be hidden: for a driver coming up A1, at the end of the walk and 20 to 40 ft down A2 by the island curb; for a driver coming down A2, by the garage doors 20 to 40 ft down A1.
  4. The installer turns and tilts the mirror until the helper shows near the middle of the mirror, not at its edge.
  5. Check from the other approach (and, with one mirror, from both). Adjust a little to share the difference.
  6. Tighten the clamp, then check once more from both driver spots.
